## What Is a Bot Blocker for Magento 2?

A bot blocker is a Magento security tool that filters out automated bot traffic before it reaches your Magento store and overloads your server.

Instead of allowing every visitor to access your website, it analyses incoming requests and automatically blocks bots with suspicious behaviour according to the configured rules while allowing real users and trusted search engine crawlers to access your store.

This helps maintain a secure, fast, and stable Magento store without manually managing firewall rules through a server or CDN.

## Why Use Magento Bot Blocker?

Not all bots accessing your store are "good". Many automated scrapers and bots continuously scan websites looking for vulnerabilities, scraping product information, creating fake accounts, generating thousands of URLs, or consuming server resources.

Without proper bot protection, Magento stores may experience:

* Increased server and hosting costs
* Slower page loading during traffic spikes
* Inflated analytics reports
* Brute-force login attempts
* Fake registrations and spam
* Excessive API and page requests
* Generation of thousands of URLs from filtered results or search

Magento 2 Bot Blocker automatically filters unwanted bot traffic before it becomes a problem.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Magento 2 Bot and Spam Blocker
### How does the Bot Blocker identify bad bots?

Magento 2 Bot Blocker identifies bad bots by scanning their activity for any unwanted actions like scraping content, attempting logins, scanning for vulnerabilities, sending spam, or consuming excessive server resources or an excessive number of requests. 

### Will the Magento Bot Blocker block Googlebot?

Magefan Bot Blocker automatically allows trusted search engine crawlers such as Googlebot and BingBot, ensuring your SEO is not affected.

### Can I block bots from specific countries with this extension?

The dedicated Magento 2 Geo Lock features in the Bot Blocker Extension allow you to deny access from selected countries.

### Can I block a specific IP address in Magento?

With the Magento Spam and Bot Blocker Extension, you can create custom blocklists using individual IP addresses, CIDR ranges, or let the extension automatically block suspicious IPs that send too many requests.

### Does blocking bots improve Magento performance?

Reducing unnecessary bot traffic in Magento lowers server load, improves response times, and helps you improve the experience for the real users who don't have to experience delays of unnecessary CAPTCHA verifications.
